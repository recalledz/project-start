13.1 Foundation

Requirement: 25,000 points

No path selected; after completion, player chooses a Path (default: Ember)

XP/sec: 0.4 × potential² × training_bonus

training_bonus: 2× when using a Training Dummy


Example: Potential = 5, 60 s/day →

0.4 × 5² = 10 XP/s
25,000 ÷ 10 = 2,500 s ≈ 41.7 minutes of work

(Adjust daily training duration for real-time estimate.)
