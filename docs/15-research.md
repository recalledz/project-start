15 – Research Trees

This section outlines the major research branches and their unlock progression.


---

15.1 Industry Tree

Miners

Unlocks the Mining skill/task.


Crafting

Enables construction of crafting facilities (e.g., Forge, Workshop).


Transmute

Unlocks the Jadefire Pavilion building.

Coal Transmute: Use the Burn Wood spell at the Jadefire Pavilion to generate coal.


Ironmancing

Unlocks transmutation of Iron Ore into Iron Plates.


Steel Transmute

Unlocks transmutation of Iron Plates and Coal into Steel.


Cores

Unlocks creation of Elemental Cores (Brazen, Earthen, Aquatic, etc.).




---

15.2 Cultivation Tree

Water Fonts

Unlocks the Water Fonts building, granting flat global Water generation and a minor increase to Max Water.


Chanting Halls

Unlocks the Chanting Halls building, enabling disciples to learn and cast spells.




---

15.3 Sect Management Tree

Foreseers

Unlocks the Exploration system and displays the in-game calendar.


Golden Reverie

Enables discovery of Gold during Coal Transmute operations.

Unlocks the Golden Mausoleum building for advanced cultivation legacy effects.
