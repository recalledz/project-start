Spell Schools & Tags

Spells are categorized into Schools (shown as tags) and Non‑school Tags:

Schools

School	Description

Voice	Influence or command others
Conjuration	Create resources; +1.05× generator potency per level
Amplification	Increase potency of existing buffs
Transference	Convert one resource into another
Destructive	Deal damage to threats
Regenerative	Restore resources or health; +1.05× regen potency per level
Blessing	Buff combat prowess
Commandment	Buff sect activities


Non‑school Tags

Single‑cast: One‑time effect, followed by a cooldown.

Generator: Produces resource over time.

Duration: Buff or effect lasts a set time.

Drain: Consumes one resource to generate another.

Combat: Only usable in dungeons.


Spell Learning & Teaching

Spells must be learned in the Library before teaching.

Cost: Attainment & Inspiration; costs scale by square of spells learned.

Opposite‑path spells cost 2×; elemental‑aligned spells cost 0.5×.

Library Menu: Learn Spell | Teach Spell.


Chanting Management

After Chanting Halls unlocks, assign idle disciples to chant.

UI shows available disciples and their chant rate (Qi/s).

Unassigned chanters free up to perform other tasks.
