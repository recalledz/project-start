14 – Crafting & Transmutation

Transmutation is performed at the Jadefire Pavilion and is a core progression school, unlocking new resources, spell components, and even advanced spells as cultivators advance. Cultivators dedicated to Transmutation gain access to exclusive crafting recipes and resource conversions.

14.2 Pavilion & Workshop Setup

Jadefire Pavilion

Cost: Softwood & Stone: 20 × (1.1^lvl) each

Effect: Enables all Transmutation spells; +3% spell potency per level


Crafting Workshop (future expansion)

Allows crafting of advanced items (weapons, tools, decorative items)

Cost & unlocks TBD based on Research Tree progression

14.3 Integration with Spells

As Transmutation unlocks, new Generator or Single-cast spells become available in the Magic schools.

Many high-tier cores and reagents used in Cultivation breakthroughs are produced exclusively via Transmutation.
