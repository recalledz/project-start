## Default Daily Routine
| Phase     | Duration (s) | Action               |
|-----------|-------------:|----------------------|
| Morning   |           60 | Meditate / Foundation |
| Midday    |           60 | Work                  |
| Afternoon |           60 | Work                  |
| Evening   |           60 | Eat                   |
| Night     |           60 | Sleep                 |

The entire day lasts five real minutes. All disciples follow this single schedule and lose food continuously. They must eat during Evening or risk hunger penalties. Use the **Schedule** button in the sect navigation to view the current phase and time remaining.

> After unlocking the Task Manager, players can customize these slots.
